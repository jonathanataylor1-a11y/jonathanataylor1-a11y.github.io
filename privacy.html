Applies to: My Friend for iPhone and iPad

The short version

My Friend runs on your device. Your conversations stay on your device.

We do not have a server. There is no account, no login, and nothing to sign up for. We cannot read your conversations, because they are never sent anywhere we could read them from. We do not use analytics.

The free app shows a small banner ad from Google AdMob under the chat. The ad component never receives your conversations, memories, or voice; what it does collect is described under "Advertising" below.

What the app stores, and where

Everything in this list is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It is included in your device backup if you back your device up, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

WhatWhyHow long
Your conversation historySo the conversation continues across sessionsUntil you clear it or delete the app
Things you asked to be rememberedSo My Friend can recall them laterUntil you ask it to forget them or delete the app
Your settingsSo the app works the way you set it upUntil changed or the app is deleted

We receive none of it. To delete it, delete the app. Inside the app you can also ask My Friend to forget a specific thing it remembered.

What leaves your device

1. Online lookups — nothing is sent unless you allow it. My Friend can check current information: game schedules and scores (ESPN), weather (Open-Meteo), news headlines (Google News) and general reference (Wikipedia). By default the app shows you exactly what it would send, and to which service, and waits for your OK. Each OK covers one lookup. If you turn "Ask before going online" off, eligible questions are looked up without asking. A lookup sends only what that service needs — a city name for weather, a date for games, a short topic for news, or a short phrase for reference. It never sends your conversation history, your memories, or anything My Friend has said. Replies that used a lookup are marked. The service receives that request and your IP address; its privacy policy governs what it does with them.

2. Advertising — a Google AdMob banner. The app shows one banner ad from Google AdMob below the conversation. To request and display ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK sends Google information about the device and the ad, not about your conversation. According to Google's own disclosure it may collect your IP address (used to estimate general location), an app- or developer-scoped device identifier, ad interactions, performance data and crash diagnostics. The app does not ask for permission to track you (Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt), so the SDK does not receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Ad requests from My Friend contain no keywords, no page content, and nothing you have typed or said. Where the law requires consent for ads (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent message before any personalised ad is requested, and Settings → Ads offers "Ad privacy choices" so you can change your answer. Google's privacy policy governs what Google does with this data: policies.google.com/privacy.

That is the complete list.

What does not leave your device

  • Your conversations. Never sent anywhere.
  • What you say to the microphone. Speech is transcribed by iOS on your device. Audio is not recorded to a file, uploaded, or kept after the words are recognised.
  • What My Friend says back. Generated on your device and spoken with your device's built-in voice.
  • Your memories. Stored and used on your device.
  • Device motion. Used to tilt the 3D scene; never stored or transmitted.
  • Any identifier of you or your device, from us. We do not create, read or send one. Google's ad SDK uses its own identifiers, described above.

The AI

My Friend's replies are generated by Apple's on-device Foundation Models (Apple Intelligence), running on your device. The app does not use Apple's Private Cloud Compute and does not send prompts to any server. On devices without Apple Intelligence, the app uses a simpler on-device fallback that also runs entirely on your device.

My Friend is an AI. Its replies can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. It isn't a person, a therapist, or a source of medical, legal or financial advice, and it can't help in an emergency. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.

Analytics and tracking

No analytics SDK. No crash reporter that receives your content. Google AdMob, as described above, is the only third party that receives anything automatically, and it never receives conversation content. We do not sell your data, and no data reaches us in the first place. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.

The app includes FluidAudio (speech), the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform SDK (banner ad and ad consent). Attribution is in the app under Settings → About → Licenses.

Children

My Friend is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect anything from anyone, of any age. Because replies are generated by an AI, their content cannot be guaranteed, and the app is rated accordingly on the App Store.

Your rights

Data-protection laws (including the GDPR and CCPA) give you rights to access, correct, export and delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none: your data is on your device, under your control, and deleting the app deletes all of it. For data Google collects through ads, use Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices where available, or Google's own controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes and the new version is published here before the version of the app it describes is released.
