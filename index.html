SupportPrivacy Policy

My Friend is a companion you can talk to, for iPhone and iPad. It runs on your device: your conversations and memories stay there, and nothing you say is sent to us.

Contact

Questions, problems or feedback: myfriendapp.support@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Does it need the internet?
No. Conversation works offline. If a question needs current information (weather, scores, news), My Friend shows you exactly what it would look up and asks first. You can change this in Settings → Online.

Why is the reply quality different on my device?
On devices with Apple Intelligence, My Friend uses Apple's on-device model. Other devices get a simpler on-device fallback. Settings shows which one is in use.

How do I make it forget something?
Ask it to forget it. You can see what it remembers in Settings → Memory → Browse memories. Deleting the app deletes everything.

How do I change ad privacy choices?
Where ad consent applies, Settings → Ads → Ad privacy choices.

The natural voice download
On first launch My Friend offers an optional download of about 100 MB for a more natural voice. Skip it and the device's built-in voice is used. You can change this in Settings → Voice.

Please remember

My Friend is an AI. Its replies are generated on your device and can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. It isn't a person, a therapist, or a source of medical, legal or financial advice, and it can't help in an emergency. If you or someone else is in danger, call your local emergency number.
